License Management

On this page

Contact ​

Send license, installation, and usage questions to ich@eleutheria.tech. The form below uploads nothing; it only assembles a message on your device and hands it to your mail app.

Open mail app
Message preview
To: ich@eleutheria.tech
Subject: [GridSubStudio] License request

Topic: License request

This form only assembles a message on your device and sends nothing to a server; your mail app sends it.

Before you write ​

  • License requests, renewals, or new computers: save your name and email under Settings → License, then copy the machine code shown there. The code is used to issue the license and must be provided again after moving to another computer.
  • Installation or feature problems: include the app version (shown in About GridSubStudio), your operating system version, and reproduction steps. For focus, scrolling, or playback sync issues, mention whether temporary diagnostics were enabled.
  • Documentation corrections: name the page and section, and include the wording you believe is correct.

Other options ​

You can also write directly to ich@eleutheria.tech with "GridSubStudio" in the subject. Only send license codes and machine codes through a channel you trust; do not post them in public issues or forums.
